A `textarea` with `data-slot="input"` in `<Dialog>` or `openDialog` now fills the width, is at least 80px tall and resizes vertically. The dark theme sets `color-scheme: dark`, so a `select`'s option list and scrollbars are dark too. The docs and playground examples mark up their content with `data-slot`, so their fields keep a visible border under Tailwind preflight, and the Styling page lists the slots.
